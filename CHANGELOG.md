# Changelog

## 1.2.2

- Activities now opens an overview: blurs the wallpaper and filters your apps as you type. Enter opens the first match, Esc or a click on empty space closes it

## 1.2.2

- Removed the Activities button from the top bar

## 1.2.1

- Search engine picker back on the search bar (DuckDuckGo default; also Google, Bing, Brave, Startpage, Ecosia, Yahoo)

## 1.2.0

- Light and dark themes (or match the system), switchable from the menu or Settings
- Press and hold an app for one second to pick it up, then drag to reorder; only the tiles you pass over slide aside
- Dock replaced by a three-line menu button in the bottom right
- Glass app tiles with fading translucent borders
- Everything is saved in extension storage and stays until you uninstall
- Save to file / Load from file for backups

## 1.1.0

GNOME-style rewrite.

- Adwaita-inspired desktop: top bar with clock and rounded dialogs
- Web apps: add by link (favicon added automatically), rename without touching the link, edit link, remove
- Search bar opens results (or a typed link) in a new tab; choose DuckDuckGo, Google, Bing or Brave
- Wallpapers: six built-in plus upload your own image
- Screensaver: animated aurora or your own uploaded video; optional idle start
- Synthesised sound effects with an on/off switch
- Firefox support (Manifest V3, Firefox 109+)

## 1.0.0

First public beta.
