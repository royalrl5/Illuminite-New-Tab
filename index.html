Illuminite New Tab

A beautifully designed new tab. Here is everything it can do.

Search

  • Type in the search bar and press Enter. It opens in a new tab.
  • Type a web address (like example.com) to open the site directly.
  • Use the engine button on the right of the bar to switch between DuckDuckGo, Google, Bing, Brave, Startpage, Ecosia and Yahoo.

Apps

  • Click Add app, enter a link, and the site's icon is added for you.
  • Right-click an app to open it in a new tab or a new window, rename it, edit its link, or remove it. Renaming never changes the link.
  • Press and hold an app for one second, then drag it to a new spot. The apps you pass over slide out of the way.

Desktop mode

  • Click the monitor button in the bottom-left corner (or choose Desktop mode in the menu). A startup animation and sound play, and the tab is renamed "New Tab (Desktop Mode)".
  • Double-click an icon to open it in a window. Drag icons to move them, or drag a box over empty desktop to select several and move or remove them together.
  • Right-click the desktop to add a shortcut where you clicked. Built-in apps: Notepad, Calculator, Clock, Paint, Task Manager, Browser and Settings.
  • Open Settings inside desktop mode for personalization, taskbar, mouse (custom colors, .cur and .ani cursors), sound, backups and a full desktop guide.

Look and feel

  • Switch between light and dark themes, or match your system, from the menu or Settings.
  • Pick a built-in wallpaper or upload your own image in Settings.
  • Sound effects can be turned on or off from the menu.

Screensaver

  • Start it from the menu. It fills the screen with a flowing animation and a clock.
  • Moving the mouse will not close it. Press Esc to exit.
  • Upload your own video in Settings, or let it start by itself after a few idle minutes.

Your data

  • Apps, order, names, wallpaper and settings are saved automatically and stay until you uninstall.
  • Save to file makes a backup. Load from file restores it. The screensaver video is not included.
